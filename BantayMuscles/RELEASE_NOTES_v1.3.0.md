## BiteMoji 1.3.0

**Accounts & sync (optional)**
- Sign in to back up your diary, profile, steps and weights — and carry them to a new phone. It's opt-in under **Profile → Sync across devices**; the app still works fully offline and signed out.

**Progress**
- New **weight trend chart** — see your logged weights over time.

**Editing**
- **Tap a logged meal** to change its servings or move it to another meal.
- **Long-press a saved food** to edit its numbers or remove it.

**Food catalog**
- More foods now sync from the shared online catalog.

---
_Updating from 1.1.0 or 1.2.0 installs normally. Only 1.0.0 needs the export → reinstall → import step (different signing key)._
