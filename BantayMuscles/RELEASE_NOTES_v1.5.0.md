## BiteMoji 1.5.0

**Your data is safer — three fixes to backup and sync**

- **Saved foods are backed up now.** My Foods was being left out of both the
  backup file and cloud sync. Restoring a backup, or signing in on a new phone,
  gave you an empty My Foods and there was no way to get those foods back. They
  travel with everything else from this version on.
- **A second phone no longer wipes the first.** Signing in used to keep whichever
  device had synced most recently and discard the other one entirely — so a day
  you logged on your phone could vanish because your tablet synced later. The two
  are now merged: anything logged on either device is kept.
- **Deletes stick.** Removing a meal or a saved food now travels between your
  devices instead of reappearing from whichever one hadn't heard about it yet.

If you've been using accounts on more than one device, this version is worth
installing on all of them.
