## BiteMoji 1.4.0

**Home-screen widget**
- Add a BiteMoji widget to your phone's home screen to see **today's calories, macros and steps** at a glance — no need to open the app.
- **Resizable:** shrink it down for just the calorie ring, or expand it for macros and steps too.
- **Tap it** to jump straight into logging a food.

To add it: long-press an empty spot on your home screen → **Widgets** → **BiteMoji** → drag it out.
