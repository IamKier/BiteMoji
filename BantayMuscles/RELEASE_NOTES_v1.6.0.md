## BiteMoji 1.6.0

**BantayMuscles is now BiteMoji.** Same app, same food list, same data — new name.

### ⚠️ Already have BantayMuscles? Move your data across
The rename also changes how Android identifies the app, so this **installs as a
separate app** rather than updating the old one. Nothing is deleted, but your
history stays behind in the old app until you bring it over. Don't uninstall
anything before step 3.

1. Open the **old BantayMuscles** app → **Profile → Backup → Export**, and copy
   the text somewhere safe (a note or a message to yourself).
2. Install this version. It appears on your home screen as **BiteMoji**, next to
   the old app.
3. Open **BiteMoji → Profile → Backup → Import** and paste the text. Check your
   diary, saved foods and weights are all there.
4. Once everything looks right, **uninstall the old BantayMuscles.**

If you sync with an account, signing in on BiteMoji restores your data too — but
export first anyway, so you have a copy that doesn't depend on the network.

New installs: just install and go.
