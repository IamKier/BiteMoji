## BantayMuscles 1.1.0

### ⚠️ Updating from 1.0.0? Save your data first
This release is signed with a new key, so it installs as a fresh app — a direct
in-app update will fail with "App not installed". To keep your data:

1. **Before updating:** open **Profile → Backup → Export** (copies all your data).
2. Paste it somewhere safe (a note or message to yourself).
3. **Uninstall** the old BantayMuscles, then install this version.
4. Open **Profile → Backup → Import** to restore everything.

New installs: just install and go.

**Smarter search**
- Finds foods even with typos or accented spellings, matches your words in any order, and ranks the closest result first.
- Your recently logged foods now appear at the top of Add — repeats are one tap.

**Tracking**
- Steps now add to your daily calorie budget, not just the steps card.
- The day rolls over at midnight automatically — no more logging to yesterday.

**Reliability**
- Barcode scanner reliably wakes back up after you switch apps.
