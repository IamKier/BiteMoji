## BiteMoji 1.2.0

Making it easy to log food that has no official database entry.

**Log anything, no database needed**
- **My Foods** — save any food you enter and reuse it with one tap. No more retyping the same home-cooked or restaurant meal.
- **Recipe builder** — combine ingredients into one dish, auto-totalled per serving, then saved to My Foods.
- **Portion guide** — eyeball amounts by hand size (palm ≈ 100g meat, fist ≈ 1 cup rice) when you have no scale or label.

**More Filipino food out of the box**
- Added ~45 common local dishes — adobo, kare-kare, kaldereta, sinigang, bulalo, arroz caldo, silog, pancit, street food (kwek-kwek, isaw, turon), and kakanin (bibingka, puto, biko, halo-halo). Curated estimates, fully searchable.

**Scanner**
- Rebuilt the barcode scanner on a newer camera engine for reliability, with a flashlight toggle for low-light scanning.

---

### Updating from 1.0.0? Save your data first
If you're still on 1.0.0, this installs as a fresh app (new signing key), so a direct update fails. Before updating: **Profile → Backup → Export**, keep the text safe, then uninstall, install this, and **Import** it back. Updating from 1.1.0 needs no such step.

_Sideloaded build — Android will ask you to confirm the install._
